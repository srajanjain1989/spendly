Privacy Policy

Your finances are personal. Here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated · October 2026

01 Data We Collect

We only collect the information needed to run Spendly:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic usage data — such as login times and browser type, used to keep the service secure and working properly.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or connect to your financial institutions.

02 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display, and summarise your expenses.
  • Generate category breakdowns and spending reports for you.
  • Send essential account emails, such as password resets.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03 Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers with access restricted to authorised personnel only. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, though no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expense records are permanently removed from our systems.

04 Third Party Services

We may rely on a small number of trusted providers — for example, hosting and email delivery services — to operate Spendly. These providers only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the fonts are requested.

05 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or would like to access, correct, or delete your data, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.
